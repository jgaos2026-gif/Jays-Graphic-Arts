## The Comparison

The universe does not operate as a simple chain: cause → effect → result.

It behaves more like vast numbers of interacting histories — matter, energy, fields, and time all contributing to each transformation.

**[INSPIRATION]** A braid captures something close to this: not merely what is connected, but what crossed, in which order, through which path, and how interactions changed the combined state.

That structural quality is what BCT borrows. Not the physics. The structure.

## What Physics Actually Supports

**[PHYSICS — Established]**  
Spacetime worldlines describe objects with persistent identity moving through time. Multiple worldlines intersecting describe interactions. Two different crossing histories can produce identical final configurations with different physical consequences.

**[PHYSICS — Established]**  
Topological quantum computing uses braid operations on non-Abelian anyons to implement quantum gates. The computation depends only on braid topology. This establishes: *a braid can be an operation, not merely a picture.*

**[PHYSICS — Theoretical, unconfirmed observationally]**  
Cosmic strings and cosmic superstrings are hypothetical topological defects. Not observed. Not claimed as evidence for BCT.

## What BCT Borrows

**[INSPIRATION — structural, not physical]**
