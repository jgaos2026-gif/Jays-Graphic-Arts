## Mathematical Backbone Summary

The following fields are directly applicable to BCT with highest priority:

1. **Artin braid groups** — the algebra BCT is built on
2. **Topological quantum computing** — established precedent for braid operations performing computation
3. **Category theory** — formal language for instruction composition
4. **Directed algebraic topology** — models ordered execution paths
5. **Tensor networks** — structured multi-strand information flow

The remaining fields provide supporting structure and comparison baselines.
