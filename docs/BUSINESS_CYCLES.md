## 1. Overview

A "business cycle" is the complete journey from initial client interest to fulfilled order and beyond. At Jays-Graphic-Arts the AI manages **eight interlocking cycles** that together cover every operational function of the company. Each cycle feeds data into the others, creating a self-reinforcing loop that improves over time.

All cycles operate simultaneously. While Cycle 3 (Production) is active for one client, Cycle 1 (Lead Acquisition) is running for prospective clients and Cycle 6 (Retention) is active for past clients. The AI orchestrates all of these concurrently.

## 2. Cycle 1 — Lead Acquisition and Qualification

**Goal**: Attract potential clients and determine whether they are a good fit before any human time is invested.

### Steps

| Step | AI Action |
|---|---|
| 1.1 Marketing | Publishes organic and paid content across channels; monitors engagement and optimises spend |
| 1.2 Inquiry capture | Receives inquiries via web form, email, or chat; creates a lead record |
| 1.3 Initial response | Sends an immediate, personalised acknowledgement within 60 seconds |
| 1.4 Discovery questionnaire | Guides the prospect through an adaptive brief questionnaire |
| 1.5 Lead scoring | Scores the lead on budget signal, brief quality, and project complexity |
| 1.6 Qualification decision | Proceeds to quoting (score ≥ 60) or sends a polite, helpful decline with alternatives (score < 60) |
| 1.7 CRM entry | Creates or updates the prospect's CRM record with all gathered information |

**Handoff to Cycle 2**: A qualified lead with a completed brief.

---

## 3. Cycle 2 — Quoting and Contracting

**Goal**: Turn a qualified lead into a signed client with clear terms, scope, and pricing.

### Steps

| Step | AI Action |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Scope analysis | Parses the brief and identifies deliverables, revision rounds, and timeline requirements |
| 2.2 Quote generation | Calculates pricing using the current rate model; produces an itemised quote document |
| 2.3 Quote delivery | Sends the branded quote with a clear validity period and acceptance link |
| 2.4 Follow-up | Sends a follow-up if no response is received within 48 hours; adjusts cadence based on engagement signals |
| 2.5 Negotiation handling | Responds to scope or pricing questions; escalates significant scope changes to human review |
| 2.6 Contract generation | Generates a customised service agreement from the accepted quote |
| 2.7 E-signature routing | Sends the contract for client e-signature via integrated signing platform |
| 2.8 Onboarding | Creates the client portal account; sends welcome pack and project kick-off instructions |

**Handoff to Cycle 3**: A signed contract with a confirmed scope, budget, and deadline.

---

## 4. Cycle 3 — Project Production

**Goal**: Produce all contracted deliverables on time and to specification.

### Steps

| Step | AI Action |
|---|---|
| 3.1 Project setup | Creates project record, assigns resources, builds milestone schedule |
| 3.2 Brief parsing | Extracts all design requirements and constraints from the contract and brief |
| 3.3 Asset generation | Runs the creative production pipeline (see [AI Capabilities § 1](AI_CAPABILITIES.md#1-creative-production)) |
| 3.4 Quality Layer 1 | Automated rule checks: brand guidelines, file specs, copy accuracy |
| 3.5 Quality Layer 2 | Independent AI self-review and similarity-to-brief scoring |
| 3.6 Confidence check | Tasks below confidence threshold are escalated before proceeding |
| 3.7 Revision iterations | Processes client revision requests; tracks versions; applies changes |
| 3.8 Final QA | Full quality sweep on the final version before delivery package is assembled |

**Handoff to Cycle 4**: A QA-passed delivery package.

---

## 5. Cycle 4 — Delivery and Approval

**Goal**: Get delivered assets formally accepted by the client.

### Steps

| Step | AI Action |
|---|---|
| 4.1 Delivery preparation | Assembles files in all required formats; creates delivery notes and asset register |
| 4.2 Secure transfer | Uploads to client portal and/or sends via secure link; notifies client |
| 4.3 Acknowledgement tracking | Monitors whether the client has opened and downloaded the delivery |
| 4.4 Follow-up | Chases unacknowledged deliveries at 24-hour intervals (up to 3 attempts) |
| 4.5 Revision requests | Processes any final revision requests within the contracted revision allowance |
| 4.6 Approval capture | Prompts client for formal written approval; records approval with timestamp |
| 4.7 Out-of-scope requests | Identifies any revision requests that exceed scope; generates a change-order quote |

**Handoff to Cycle 5**: A client-approved delivery with signed approval record.

---

## 6. Cycle 5 — Billing and Collections

**Goal**: Collect payment in full, on time, in compliance with all financial regulations.

### Steps

| Step | AI Action |
|---|---|
| 5.1 Invoice generation | Creates an itemised invoice based on the contract and any approved change orders |
| 5.2 Tax calculation | Applies correct tax rate for the client's billing jurisdiction |
| 5.3 Invoice delivery | Sends invoice via email and client portal simultaneously |
| 5.4 Payment reminder sequence | Sends reminders at due date, 3 days overdue, 7 days overdue |
| 5.5 Payment processing | Processes card, ACH, or bank transfer payments through integrated gateways |
| 5.6 Payment matching | Reconciles received payments against open invoices; updates account balances |
| 5.7 Receipt issuance | Sends payment confirmation and receipt automatically |
| 5.8 Overdue escalation | Escalates invoices >30 days overdue to human review for collections decision |
| 5.9 Bookkeeping | Records all transactions in the accounting ledger with correct categorisation |

**Handoff to Cycle 6**: A paid, closed project with full financial records.

---

## 7. Cycle 6 — Post-Project and Retention

**Goal**: Turn a satisfied client into a repeat client and brand advocate.

### Steps

| Step | AI Action |
|---|---|
| 6.1 Satisfaction survey | Sends a short post-project survey 3 days after final payment |
| 6.2 Feedback analysis | Analyses survey responses; flags low scores for human follow-up |
| 6.3 Testimonial request | Requests a testimonial or review from satisfied clients (score ≥ 4/5) |
| 6.4 Portfolio update | Adds approved work to the public portfolio with client permission |
| 6.5 Check-in schedule | Schedules periodic check-in communications (30 days, 90 days, 6 months) |
| 6.6 Upsell identification | Analyses client history and flags relevant new service opportunities |
| 6.7 Loyalty offers | Generates personalised offers for repeat engagements |
| 6.8 Referral programme | Manages referral codes and tracks inbound referrals back to their source |

**Loop**: Satisfied clients re-enter Cycle 1 as warm leads for future projects.

## 8. Cycle 7 — Internal Operations and Administration

**Goal**: Keep the business infrastructure running smoothly so all other cycles operate without interruption.

### Activities

| Area | AI Activity |
|---|---|
| System monitoring | 24/7 health checks on all services; automated alerting and incident response |
| Security | Weekly dependency scans, automatic patch application, access-log review |
| Vendor management | Tracks SLAs with all vendors; flags renewals; benchmarks pricing |
| Software licensing | Monitors all subscriptions; handles renewals and cancellations |
| Document management | Organises and archives all business documents; applies retention policies |
| Compliance monitoring | Ongoing regulatory checks (see [Compliance Framework](COMPLIANCE.md)) |
| HR administration | Manages contractor agreements, invoices, and payment records |
| Infrastructure scaling | Adjusts compute resources to match production load; controls costs |

---

## 9. Cycle 8 — Continuous Improvement

**Goal**: Make every subsequent business cycle faster, cheaper, and higher quality than the last.

### Activities

| Activity | Frequency |
|---|---|
| KPI review and reporting | Monthly |
| Revision pattern analysis → prompt updates | After every 50 projects |
| Client feedback → quality threshold adjustment | After every satisfaction survey batch |
| Failed-task retrospective → regression test addition | Within 48 hours of any escalated failure |
| Model performance benchmarking | Monthly |
| Industry trend library update | Quarterly |
| External compliance audit | Annual |

All improvement changes go through the model versioning and canary-deployment process described in [AI Reliability § 6](AI_RELIABILITY.md#6-model-versioning-and-rollback).

## 11. Key Performance Indicators

The following KPIs are tracked across all business cycles and reviewed monthly:

| KPI | Target | Measurement Point |
|---|---|---|
| Lead-to-quote conversion rate | ≥ 70% | Cycle 1 → Cycle 2 |
| Quote-to-contract conversion rate | ≥ 50% | Cycle 2 → Cycle 3 |
| On-time delivery rate | ≥ 95% | Cycle 3 → Cycle 4 |
| First-pass approval rate (no revisions) | ≥ 60% | Cycle 4 |
| Invoice collection rate within 30 days | ≥ 90% | Cycle 5 |
| Client satisfaction score (average) | ≥ 4.5 / 5 | Cycle 6 |
| Repeat business rate | ≥ 40% of closed projects | Cycle 6 |
| Average cycle time (brief to delivery) | ≤ 5 business days | Cycles 3–4 |
| AI task escalation rate | ≤ 5% | All cycles |
| Compliance incident rate | 0 | All cycles |
