## 1. Creative Production

The AI handles the full creative pipeline for graphic design deliverables.

### Design Generation
- Generates logo concepts, brand identity systems, and style guides from client briefs
- Creates print-ready materials: flyers, brochures, business cards, banners, posters, packaging
- Produces digital assets: social media graphics, email templates, web banners, app icons
- Generates motion graphics and animated social content (GIF, MP4, WebM)
- Adapts any asset to multiple sizes and formats from a single master file

### Typography and Color
- Selects typeface pairings consistent with brand brief and industry conventions
- Applies color theory to build harmonious palettes from a seed color or brand brief
- Checks all color combinations for WCAG accessibility compliance

### File Production
- Exports in all professional formats: AI, EPS, PDF, SVG, PNG, JPEG, TIFF, PSD
- Generates print-ready files with bleed, crop marks, and correct color profiles (CMYK/Pantone)
- Packages projects with linked assets and font summaries for handoff to print vendors

### Revision Handling
- Interprets natural-language revision requests ("make the logo bolder", "shift the palette cooler")
- Tracks revision history and can restore any previous version of an asset
- Generates a side-by-side comparison document for client approval

## 2. Client Communication and Relationship Management

### Intake and Onboarding
- Runs an AI-driven discovery questionnaire to capture brand voice, audience, budget, and timeline
- Generates a project scope document and terms of service for client review and e-signature
- Creates a client portal account and sends branded welcome communications

### Ongoing Communication
- Answers client questions 24/7 via email, web chat, and messaging integrations
- Sends proactive status updates at every project milestone
- Handles revision requests, clarifications, and scope-change discussions
- Translates communications into 40+ languages

### Relationship Management
- Maintains a CRM record of every client interaction, preference, and past project
- Identifies upsell and cross-sell opportunities based on client history and project patterns
- Sends automated check-in messages to lapsed clients with personalized offers
- Manages client satisfaction surveys and synthesizes feedback into actionable reports

## 3. Order and Project Management

### Project Intake
- Parses client briefs from email, forms, or chat and creates structured project records
- Assigns a project code, sets milestones, and estimates delivery dates using historical data
- Identifies dependencies and flags resource conflicts before work begins

### Workflow Orchestration
- Schedules tasks across the production pipeline in priority order
- Allocates computational resources to meet deadline targets
- Monitors task progress in real time and adjusts schedules dynamically if a task falls behind
- Manages concurrent projects without conflicts between client assets or configurations

### Delivery Management
- Prepares delivery packages and generates client-facing delivery notes
- Sends files via secure download link, cloud drive integration, or direct transfer to vendor
- Tracks delivery confirmation and follows up on unacknowledged deliveries automatically
- Manages approval workflows: routes deliverables for sign-off and records approvals

## 4. Financial Management

### Quoting and Invoicing
- Generates accurate quotes from project scope using a configurable pricing model
- Produces branded, itemised invoices with correct tax calculations for client jurisdiction
- Handles multi-currency pricing with live exchange-rate conversion

### Payment Processing
- Sends payment reminders on schedule and escalates overdue invoices automatically
- Processes payments through integrated payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, ACH/BACS)
- Applies payments to open invoices and updates account balances in real time
- Issues receipts and refund confirmations automatically

### Expense and Budget Management
- Tracks project-level costs and compares actuals against budget
- Categorises all business expenses and maps them to accounting codes
- Flags budget overruns before they occur based on projected spend

### Bookkeeping and Reconciliation
- Reconciles bank transactions against invoices and purchase orders daily
- Generates profit-and-loss, cash-flow, and balance-sheet reports on demand
- Prepares data exports compatible with QuickBooks, Xero, and FreshBooks
- Produces VAT/GST/sales-tax return summaries ready for accountant review

## 6. Quality Assurance

See [AI Reliability](AI_RELIABILITY.md) for full details on the three-layer QA pipeline. In summary, the AI performs:

- Automated brand-guideline compliance checks on every asset
- Resolution, color-space, and file-format validation
- Copy accuracy and spell-check for all client-facing text
- Trademark and copyright keyword scanning
- Independent second-pass review by a separate quality model
- Final confidence scoring with escalation for any score below threshold

## 7. Legal and Intellectual Property Management

### Contract Management
- Generates customised service agreements, NDAs, and licensing contracts from templates
- Routes contracts for e-signature and tracks completion status
- Stores executed contracts with searchable metadata and expiry alerts

### IP and Copyright
- Scans generated designs against known trademark databases before delivery
- Tracks licensed font and stock-asset usage to ensure licence scope compliance
- Generates IP ownership transfer documentation on project completion
- Monitors the web for unauthorized use of client-owned work (via reverse-image search integrations)

### Regulatory Compliance
- Keeps an up-to-date checklist of jurisdiction-specific regulations (GDPR, CCPA, POPIA, etc.)
- Applies appropriate data-handling procedures based on client and asset geography
- Generates data-processing agreements for clients who require them
- Produces compliance status reports on request

## 11. Self-Improvement and Learning

The AI continuously improves through structured feedback loops:

- **Client feedback integration**: Scores and written feedback are used to fine-tune quality thresholds and prompt templates
- **Revision pattern analysis**: Frequent revision requests of the same type trigger prompt engineering updates to reduce that revision class
- **Error retrospectives**: Every escalated or failed task is reviewed and, where possible, converted into a regression test case
- **Industry monitoring**: The system monitors design trend publications and updates its style reference library quarterly

All model updates follow the versioning and rollback procedures described in [AI Reliability](AI_RELIABILITY.md#6-model-versioning-and-rollback).

## 12. Capability Limits and Boundaries

The AI operates within clearly defined boundaries. The following situations always involve a human:

| Situation | Reason |
|---|---|
| Bespoke legal advice | Requires a qualified attorney |
| Formal tax filing | Requires a licensed accountant signature |
| Verbal negotiation with clients | Complex interpersonal judgement |
| Crisis communications | Sensitive, high-stakes brand decisions |
| New service-line design decisions | Strategic human direction required |
| Any task with confidence score below threshold | See [AI Reliability § 3](AI_RELIABILITY.md#3-human-oversight-triggers) |

These boundaries exist to protect clients, ensure legal compliance, and maintain the quality bar that the company was built on.
