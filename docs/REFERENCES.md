# References

This document lists prior work that informs the BCT research program. All cited works are established. None are claimed as original contributions of this project.

---

## Foundational Mathematics

**Artin, E. (1925).** Theorie der Zöpfe. *Abhandlungen aus dem Mathematischen Seminar der Universität Hamburg*, 4(1), 47–72.  
*The original definition of braid groups.*

**Artin, E. (1947).** Theory of Braids. *Annals of Mathematics*, 48(1), 101–126.  
*English-language treatment of braid group theory.*

**Birman, J. S. (1974).** *Braids, Links, and Mapping Class Groups.* Princeton University Press.  
*Comprehensive treatment of braid theory and its connections to knot theory.*

**Kassel, C., & Turaev, V. (2008).** *Braid Groups.* Springer.  
*Modern reference for braid group theory.*

---

## Algebraic Topology

**Hatcher, A. (2002).** *Algebraic Topology.* Cambridge University Press. (Available free at https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATpage.html)  
*Standard reference for algebraic topology.*

**Munkres, J. R. (1984).** *Elements of Algebraic Topology.* Addison-Wesley.

---

## Directed Algebraic Topology

**Grandis, M. (2009).** *Directed Algebraic Topology: Models of Non-Reversible Worlds.* Cambridge University Press.  
*Foundational reference for directed topology, directly relevant to ordered execution path modeling.*

**Fajstrup, L., Goubault, E., Haucourt, E., Mimram, S., & Raussen, M. (2016).** *Directed Algebraic Topology and Concurrency.* Springer.

---

## Category Theory

**Mac Lane, S. (1998).** *Categories for the Working Mathematician* (2nd ed.). Springer.  
*Standard reference. Relevant for compositional semantics of braid instructions.*

**Awodey, S. (2010).** *Category Theory* (2nd ed.). Oxford University Press.

**Baez, J. C., & Stay, M. (2011).** Physics, Topology, Logic and Computation: A Rosetta Stone. In *New Structures for Physics*, Springer.  
*Connecting category theory to physical and computational structures.*

---

## Knot Theory

**Adams, C. C. (2004).** *The Knot Book.* American Mathematical Society.  
*Accessible introduction to knot theory and braid closures.*

**Lickorish, W. B. R. (1997).** *An Introduction to Knot Theory.* Springer.

---

## Topological Quantum Computing

**Kitaev, A. (2003).** Fault-tolerant quantum computation by anyons. *Annals of Physics*, 303(1), 2–30.  
*[INSPIRATION source] Foundational paper on topological quantum computing using braid operations.*

**Freedman, M., Kitaev, A., Larsen, M., & Wang, Z. (2003).** Topological quantum computation. *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society*, 40(1), 31–38.  
*[INSPIRATION source] Braid operations as quantum gates in topological quantum computing.*

**Nayak, C., Simon, S. H., Stern, A., Freedman, M., & Das Sarma, S. (2008).** Non-Abelian anyons and topological quantum computation. *Reviews of Modern Physics*, 80(3), 1083.

---

## Automata and Computation Theory

**Sipser, M. (2012).** *Introduction to the Theory of Computation* (3rd ed.). Cengage.  
*Standard reference for Turing completeness, automata, and formal language theory.*

**Hopcroft, J. E., Motwani, R., & Ullman, J. D. (2006).** *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation* (3rd ed.). Pearson.

---

## Petri Nets

**Petri, C. A. (1962).** *Kommunikation mit Automaten.* PhD thesis, Universität Hamburg.  
*Original definition of Petri nets — comparison baseline for BCT concurrency model.*

**Reisig, W. (2013).** *Understanding Petri Nets.* Springer.

---

## Tensor Networks

**Orús, R. (2014).** A practical introduction to tensor networks: Matrix product states and projected entangled pair states. *Annals of Physics*, 349, 117–158.  
*Relevant for multi-strand information flow formalization.*

**Biamonte, J., & Love, P. (2011).** Realizable Hamiltonians for universal adiabatic quantum computers. *Physical Review A*, 78(1).

---

## Persistent Homology

**Edelsbrunner, H., Letscher, D., & Zomorodian, A. (2002).** Topological persistence and simplification. *Discrete and Computational Geometry*, 28(4), 511–533.  
*Foundational paper on persistent homology.*

**Carlsson, G. (2009).** Topology and data. *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society*, 46(2), 255–308.

---

## Lattice Theory and Order

**Davey, B. A., & Priestley, H. A. (2002).** *Introduction to Lattices and Order* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press.  
*Relevant for partial order structure of authority and trust hierarchies.*

---

## Information Theory

**Shannon, C. E. (1948).** A mathematical theory of communication. *Bell System Technical Journal*, 27(3), 379–423.  
*Foundational reference for bounds on information preservation.*

**Cover, T. M., & Thomas, J. A. (2006).** *Elements of Information Theory* (2nd ed.). Wiley.

---

## Fault Tolerance and Recovery

**Lamport, L. (1978).** Time, clocks, and the ordering of events in a distributed system. *Communications of the ACM*, 21(7), 558–565.  
*Fundamental reference for ordering in distributed systems.*

**Gray, J., & Reuter, A. (1992).** *Transaction Processing: Concepts and Techniques.* Morgan Kaufmann.  
*Comprehensive treatment of recovery and durability in computing systems.*

---

## Provenance

**Moreau, L., & Missier, P. (Eds.) (2013).** PROV-DM: The PROV Data Model. W3C Recommendation.  
*Standard data model for computational provenance.*

**Buneman, P., Khanna, S., & Tan, W.-C. (2001).** Why and where: A characterization of data provenance. In *Proceedings of ICDT 2001*.
