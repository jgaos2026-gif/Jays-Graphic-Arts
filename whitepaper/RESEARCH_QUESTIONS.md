# Research Questions

**Author:** John E. Arenz — JGA Enterprises, Mendota, Illinois

---

## Primary Research Questions

1. Can braid topology preserve execution history better than conventional state machines for computation classes where history preservation is mandatory?

2. Can executable braid operations improve recovery fidelity compared to checkpoint-based approaches?

3. Can braid architectures achieve higher verification completeness than post-hoc verification for intermediate state violations?

4. Can braid instruction families become a practical ISA with acceptable overhead on conventional hardware?

5. Can AI reasoning systems benefit from braid-based provenance and authority structures?

## Secondary Research Questions

6. Is the executable braid model Turing-complete? (Open Problem OP-1)

7. Can braid invariants (Jones polynomial) classify computational equivalence? (Open Problem OP-3)

8. What is the correct categorical formalization of executable braid composition? (Open Problem OP-4)

9. What is the relationship between executable braids and Petri nets? (Open Problem OP-5)

10. Can the braid relation be correctly incorporated into the tamper detection engine without false positives? (Open Problem OP-9)
