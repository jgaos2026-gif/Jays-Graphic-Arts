How It Works

Brief in. Artwork out. That simple.

Every project moves through an automated eight-cycle pipeline that handles everything from your first inquiry to your final invoice.

01

Submit Your Brief

Fill in our adaptive questionnaire — it captures your brand, audience, budget, and timeline, then prepares a structured brief you can send directly to our team.

02

Sign & Kick Off

Accept the quote, e-sign the service agreement, and the AI immediately spins up a project record, assigns resources, and begins production.

03

AI Production & QA

Your assets pass through three independent quality layers — rule checks, AI self-review, and confidence scoring — before you ever see them.

04

Review & Approve

Files land in your secure client portal. Request revisions in plain English. Once you approve, the AI issues your invoice and closes the project automatically.

AI Reliability

Engineered to be trustworthy

Our platform is built on fail-safe defaults, minimal authority, and continuous verification. Every decision the AI makes is logged, scored, and auditable.

Three-Layer Quality Control
Rule checks → AI self-review → confidence thresholding. Any output scoring below 85 is automatically escalated to human review.
Immutable Audit Logs
Every AI action is recorded with who, what, when, why, and a SHA-256 hash — retained for 7 years. Full auditability on request.
Canary Deployments & Auto-Rollback
New model versions are canary-tested on 5% of traffic before full promotion. Automatic rollback fires if error rates rise above baseline.
