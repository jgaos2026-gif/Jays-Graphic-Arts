Featured Case Study

Nova Tech — Full Brand System

From a 10-minute brief to a complete, investor-ready brand identity — delivered in 3 days with zero revision requests.

3 days
Total delivery time
The Brief

Nova Tech needed a brand identity from scratch before their Series A announcement. Budget: $850. Timeline: under one week. Requirements: a mark that felt "technical but human, confident without being corporate."

The Outcome

Three logo concepts generated within 4 hours of brief submission. Client selected concept two with one minor tweak (requested in plain English). Full brand system delivered 64 hours after kick-off. First-pass approval — no additional revisions.

Deliverables
Logo suite (primary, horizontal, icon-only)
Color palette with accessibility scores
Typography system (display + body pairing)
12-page brand style guide (PDF)
Business card & letterhead templates
Social media profile & cover templates
All source files (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, PDF)
QA Summary

Trademark scan: no conflicts found in USPTO, EUIPO, or WIPO databases. All color combinations passed WCAG 2.1 AA contrast checks. Confidence score on final delivery: 96/100.
