# Governance

**Author:** John E. Arenz — JGA Enterprises, Mendota, Illinois

## Project Lead

John E. Arenz, JGA Enterprises, Mendota, Illinois, is the sole decision-maker for this research project at version 0.x.

## Contribution Process

See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for contribution guidelines.

## Decision Making

For v0.x (research prototype phase):

- Architecture decisions: project lead
- Claim classifications: project lead; community may challenge via issues
- Hypothesis status updates: requires documented evidence; community may propose
- Law changes: not permitted; the ten governing laws are immutable by design

## Version Governance

- `0.x` releases: project lead discretion
- `1.0` release: requires formal semantics complete and at least one external review
- `2.0` release: requires independent reproduction of at least one H1–H5 benchmark

## Claims Dispute Process

If you believe a claim is misclassified (e.g., something labeled HYPOTHESIS is actually ESTABLISHED, or vice versa):

1. Open an issue with label `claims-dispute`
2. Provide evidence (citation, proof, or counter-example)
3. Project lead reviews and updates `CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` within 30 days

## Research Integrity

This project follows the principles in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. Overclaiming is treated as seriously as underclaiming. All hypothesis → result promotions require documented evidence.
