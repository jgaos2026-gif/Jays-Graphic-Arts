Privacy Policy

How the public site handles project inquiries and website data.

1. Information you provide

When you use the contact form, the details you enter stay on your device until you choose to send the generated email to hello@jays-graphic-arts.ai.

2. How inquiry data is used

We use inquiry details to review your request, communicate about the project, prepare estimates, and deliver services you request. We do not sell inquiry data.

3. Website analytics and logs

Basic hosting and network logs may be collected by the platform serving this website for security, performance, and availability purposes.

4. Data sharing

We share data only when necessary to operate the website, respond to your request, comply with law, or protect the business and its users.

5. Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of inquiry information we hold by emailing hello@jays-graphic-arts.ai.

6. Updates

We may update this policy as the website and services evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
