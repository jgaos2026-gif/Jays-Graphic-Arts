Privacy Policy

How the public site handles project inquiries and website data.

1. Information you provide

When you submit the project form, the site attempts to send the information you entered to the Jays-Graphic-Arts project-intake backend so we can create and track your inquiry. This may include your name, email address, company, requested service, budget range, timeline, referral source, and project brief. If automated intake is unavailable, the site offers an email fallback to hello@jays-graphic-arts.ai instead.

2. How inquiry data is used

We use inquiry details to review and route your request, communicate with you, prepare estimates, manage project workflow, maintain operational and audit records, and deliver services you request. We do not sell inquiry data.

3. Data boundaries and operational records

Customer and project details are intended to remain inside the Brick 1 business-data boundary. Governance and integrity systems may receive identifiers, hashes, status references, or verification results needed to supervise workflow without requiring the full customer brief or artwork to enter the Sovereign Stitch proof spine.

4. Website analytics and logs

Basic hosting, network, security, rate-limit, and availability logs may be processed by the services used to host the website and project-intake backend. These logs are used for security, abuse prevention, reliability, and troubleshooting.

5. Data sharing

We share or process data with service providers only as needed to operate the website and project workflow, respond to your request, fulfill an authorized service, comply with law, or protect the business and its users. Payment, printing, shipping, CRM, or other external providers are used only when their corresponding workflow is enabled and required for the service.

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of inquiry information we hold by emailing hello@jays-graphic-arts.ai. Some records may need to be retained when required for legal, accounting, security, dispute-resolution, or audit purposes.

7. Updates

We may update this policy as the website, backend, connectors, and services evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
