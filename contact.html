FAQ

Common questions

How does submitting my brief work?

As soon as you submit the form, your device opens a ready-to-send email draft addressed to us. That means your full request is in your hands immediately, and we can reply by email after review.

What if I'm not happy with the first draft?

Every project includes revision rounds (2 for Starter, 4 for Studio). Request changes in plain English — "make the palette warmer", "bolder font" — and the AI revises within hours.

Who owns the final artwork?

You do — 100%. Upon final payment, all intellectual property rights transfer to you in full. We retain no licence to reuse your work. This is documented in every service agreement.

Is my brief kept confidential?

Yes. Brief details are encrypted in transit and at rest. We offer NDAs for all Studio and Enterprise projects. We never share client information with third parties without consent.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Yes — we work with clients worldwide. Invoices are issued in your local currency with correct tax (VAT, GST, sales tax) applied automatically. Communications are available in 40+ languages.

What file formats will I receive?

All source files (AI, EPS, SVG, PSD) plus export formats for every intended use (PDF, PNG, JPEG, TIFF, WebM, MP4). Print files include correct bleed and color profiles. No extra charge for additional formats.
